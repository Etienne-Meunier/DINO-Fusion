\section*{Summary}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Goal: generate the post-spin-up $(T, S)$ state of the Veros ACC channel from the EKE-closure
        coefficients $(c_k, c_\epsilon)$, skipping the spin-up.
  \item Pipeline: DINO-Fusion diffusion code adapted to a parameter ensemble; one split runs end to end in
        under one A100 hour.
  \item Hold-out RMSE 0.061 / 0.076 / 0.064 / \SI{0.141}{K} (scattered / band of three rows / centre block of
        49 runs / top row), against 0.037 / 0.116 / 0.114 / \SI{0.170}{K} for the nearest training run and
        0.155 / 0.197 / 0.197 / \SI{0.470}{K} for the mean state: the model wins in the three large-gap splits,
        by a factor 1.8 with half the grid held out, and loses only to one-step neighbours. A Wasserstein metric
        on the stratification profiles gives the same ranking.
  \item Main lesson: DINO's constraint that re-imposes exact zeros on land and padding after every sampling step
        cost 0.03--\SI{0.09}{K} (0.152 / 0.135 / 0.136 / \SI{0.173}{K} before). Re-imposing them at the step's noise level,
        as in training, fixes it without retraining.
  \item Physical miss: temperature inversions are generated at the rate of the weakly eddying runs in every
        regime.
\end{itemize}

\section{Data}
\begin{itemize}
  \item 100 Veros ACC runs on a $10\times10$ log-spaced grid, $c_k\in[0.0125,\,0.8]$,
        $c_\epsilon\in[0.0875,\,5.6]$; 100 years, 30-day output; interior grid $30\times42\times15$ (x, y, z).
  \item Kept: $T$ and $S$, last 20 years (241 snapshots per run). $S = \SI{35}{psu}$ everywhere in every run.
  \item Nearly deterministic end states: within-window std \SI{0.01}{K} above \SI{1400}{m} vs
        \SI{0.05}{K} between grid neighbours; the bottom level still drifts by \SI{0.45}{K} over the window.
  \item One effective parameter: $\log c_k - 1.05\,\log c_\epsilon$ explains 99\,\% of the variance of the
        domain-mean $T$; the low-$c_k/c_\epsilon$ part of the grid saturates.
  \item Per-level distribution of $T$ (Figure~\ref{fig:tdist}): above \SI{400}{m} a mixture of restoring
        values and interior, with $\sigma_z$ set by the meridional gradient, so $\mu_z \pm 3\sigma_z$ is wider than
        the level and the normalised values stay within $[-0.85,\,0.6]$; below \SI{1400}{m} a warm tail to
        $x' = +2.7$. Run-to-run signal $\sigma_z^{\mathrm{runs}}$: 0.7\,\% of $3\sigma_z$ at the surface, 26\,\% at
        the bottom.
\end{itemize}

\section{Method}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Fields $\to$ 30 channels $(T_{1..15}, S_{1..15})$, normalised per level, land $\to 0$,
        zero-padded $42\times30 \to 48\times32$:
        \begin{equation}
          x'_{z} = \frac{x_{z} - \mu_{z}}{3\,\max(\sigma_{z}, 0.05)} .
          \label{eq:norm}
        \end{equation}
        $\mu_z, \sigma_z$ are computed once on all 100 runs and held fixed for every split (a deliberate, mild
        leakage of 30 scaling constants; no held-out state enters training).
  \item Denoiser: UNet2DModel (64, 64, 128, 128), 6.9\,M parameters. The condition $c$, the standardised pair
        $(\log c_k, \log c_\epsilon)$, enters through an MLP added to the timestep embedding.
  \item DDPM, $\epsilon$-prediction, 1000 steps, squared-cosine schedule, EMA:
        \begin{equation}
          \mathcal L(\theta) = \mathbb E_{t,\,x_0,\,\epsilon}\,
          \big\| \epsilon - \epsilon_\theta\big(\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon,\; t,\; c\big) \big\|^2 .
        \end{equation}
        20\,000 steps, batch 32, AdamW $10^{-4}$ with cosine decay; \SI{22}{min} on one A100.
  \item Sampling: 1000-step DDPM loop with EMA weights; the predicted clean state is clipped at $|x'| \le 1$;
        after every step the land and padding cells are reset to zero at the step's noise level,
        $\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,z$, as in training. 32 samples per condition.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Differences from the DINO-Fusion code.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Data: a parameter ensemble of 100 runs held in memory and split by run, instead of one DINO trajectory
        streamed from webdataset tars; no \texttt{tensordict}.
  \item Conditioning: the UNet receives $(\log c_k, \log c_\epsilon)$ through the timestep embedding, with optional
        classifier-free dropout; DINO's UNet is unconditional and is steered only by sampling-time constraints.
  \item Transforms: the same concatenate--normalise--mask--pad chain and the same 3-std normalisation, but no
        vertical striding (all 15 levels kept), padding $(1,1,3,3)$ for the $42\times30$ grid, and statistics
        fixed on all runs.
  \item Training: the same DDPM settings, run step-based on one GPU with a cosine schedule and checkpoint
        resume, instead of DINO's epoch loop under \texttt{accelerate}.
  \item Sampling: the same DDPM loop and constraint hook; DINO's BorderZeroConstraint (exact zeros) is replaced
        by zeros at the step's noise level; DINO's stratification constraints are not ported yet.
  \item Added: hold-out splits, baselines, and the $W_1$ metric on stratification profiles.
\end{itemize}

\section{Evaluation}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Truth: 20-year time-mean state of each held-out run; metrics on water cells.
  \item Baselines: mean training state; nearest training run in standardised log-parameter space; average of
        the axis-neighbour training runs on the grid.
  \item Four hold-out splits, same data file and statistics, different training sets: scattered (10 interior
        points), band of three rows ($c_k$ = 0.126, 0.2, 0.3175; 30 runs; training rows a factor 6.3 apart),
        centre block (the $7\times7$ centre of the grid, $c_k$ 0.0315--0.504, $c_\epsilon$ 0.22--3.5; 49 runs;
        training on the outer rows and columns only), top row ($c_k$ = 0.8; 10 runs; extrapolation).
  \item Distributional metric: each state is reduced to its profile of horizontal means over water cells,
        $\bar T_z$; per level, the Wasserstein-1 distance between the 32 generated values and the 241 true
        snapshots of the window, through the quantile functions,
        \begin{equation}
          W_1^{(z)} = \int_0^1 \big| F_{g,z}^{-1}(q) - F_{t,z}^{-1}(q) \big|\, dq, \qquad
          W_1 = \sum_z \frac{\Delta z_z}{H}\, W_1^{(z)} ,
        \end{equation}
        with $\Delta z_z$ the level thicknesses ($H = \SI{2080}{m}$). All 241 snapshots are used: the quantile
        form accepts unequal sizes and a subsample of 32 would only add noise. A point prediction $x$ (the
        baselines) gives $W_1^{(z)} = \frac{1}{241}\sum_i |x_z - \bar T_{z,i}|$. $W_1$ rewards a correct spread and
        does not reward collapse to the mean; the RMSE keeps the horizontal structure.
\end{itemize}

\section{Findings}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The land/padding constraint was the main error source.} Same models, sampling only: exact zeros
        after every step give 0.152 / 0.135 / 0.136 / \SI{0.173}{K}; zeros at the step's noise level give
        0.061 / 0.076 / 0.064 / \SI{0.141}{K}. In training those cells carry noise like every other, so a solid block of
        zeros at high noise levels is out of distribution. The gain is largest at the bottom
        (0.34 $\to$ \SI{0.07}{K}, scattered); the mean-state collapse of the first version was this artefact.
  \item \textbf{Model vs baselines.} Band: beats the nearest run at every level below \SI{26}{m} and in all
        three rows (0.063 / 0.074 / \SI{0.091}{K} vs 0.063 / 0.132 / \SI{0.152}{K} for $c_k$ = 0.126 / 0.2 / 0.3175).
        Centre block: beats the nearest run at every level below the surface; over the block the error is flat,
        0.04--\SI{0.12}{K} (centre $3\times3$ \SI{0.068}{K}, rim \SI{0.063}{K}), where the nearest run degrades from
        \SI{0.10}{K} at the rim to \SI{0.18}{K} at the centre; it wins at the 28 runs where the state changes with the
        parameters (nearest run 0.10--\SI{0.39}{K}) and loses by at most \SI{0.07}{K} at the 21 runs of the flat regime,
        where a copied neighbour is already within \SI{0.05}{K} (Figure~\ref{fig:block}).
        Top row: beats the nearest run at every level but the surface and the bottom, and beats copying the row
        below for $c_\epsilon$ 0.14--1.4 (0.05--0.26 vs 0.08--\SI{0.31}{K}); loses at the corner (0.41 vs
        \SI{0.35}{K}) and in the flat regime ($c_\epsilon \ge 3.5$). Scattered: loses to the one-step neighbours
        (0.061 vs 0.037 / \SI{0.020}{K}) except between 490 and \SI{650}{m} (Figures~\ref{fig:scat}--\ref{fig:top}).
  \item \textbf{Depth structure.} 0.04--\SI{0.08}{K} at every level in the scattered and block splits; the
        bottom levels are no longer special (\SI{0.07}{K}) except in the top row (\SI{0.29}{K}, the corner runs). Worst band:
        the top \SI{100}{m}, where $3\sigma_z \approx \SI{12}{K}$ amplifies sampler noise.
  \item \textbf{The clip is a regulariser, not a range limit.} Same models, exact zeros, scattered / top:
        clip at $|x'| \le 1$ gives 0.152 / \SI{0.173}{K}, at 1.5 0.194 / \SI{0.183}{K}, at 3 0.302 / \SI{0.277}{K},
        although the final samples barely exceed $|x'| = 1$ (0.1\,\% of values): clipping the early clean-state
        estimates keeps the chain on track. Its price is a range limit: 12\,\% of the held-out top row's true
        bottom values lie above $\mu_z + 3\sigma_z$ and cannot be generated; the corner run is the largest top-row
        error.
  \item \textbf{Inversions.} 10--12\,\% of interfaces with $\partial_z T < 0$ in every regime; truth 7.7\,\%
        (scattered), 4.2\,\% (block), 1.3\,\% (band), 0.1\,\% (top). Not learned.
  \item \textbf{$W_1$ agrees with the RMSE ranking.} Training grid points score like held-out ones (0.083 vs
        \SI{0.071}{K}, scattered); from \SI{490}{m} down the samples beat the point baselines in the band and top
        splits (0.058 vs \SI{0.115}{K} at \SI{650}{m}, band), which pay for the spread they cannot represent.
  \item \textbf{Plumbing.} $S$ within \SI{0.02}{psu} of 35 with no constraint; land exact; ensemble spread
        $4\times$ the true within-window spread (Figures~\ref{fig:samples}--\ref{fig:samples_top}).
\end{itemize}

\section{Next steps}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Stratification constraint at sampling time (DINO's isotonic projection on $T$): targets the inversions.
  \item Top-row corner: the clip's range limit is now the dominant extrapolation error; a clip that follows the
        conditioning (bounds from the nearest training rows) is the next sampling-only test.
  \item Stronger conditioning: classifier-free guidance; the year as a third condition (bottom drift of
        \SI{0.45}{K} inside the window). A second training seed to size the seed noise.
  \item Evaluate against snapshots as well as the time mean (nearest-snapshot RMSE, spread--skill).
  \item Then the same pipeline on a global Veros configuration (active $S$, seasonal cycle, multi-century spin-up).
\end{itemize}
